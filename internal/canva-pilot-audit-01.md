## Verified content findings

- Slide 5 has the requested **12 / 8 / 4** illustrative figures, but also adds **80% / 67% / 50% of target** with no targets supplied. Remove the percentages or add clearly identified hypothetical target values with internally consistent calculations.
- The disclaimer on slide 5 reads **'Fictional demonstration – Ilustrative figures'**; correct to **'Fictional demonstration — illustrative figures'**.
- Multiple missing spaces and typos, including 'NorthstarAnalytics', 'fromthe', 'datathan', 'critical issuesin', 'asingle', 'keymetrics', 'Take timelyaction', 'regulardecision', 'Metic', and 'In Track'.
- The brief required an illustrative disclaimer on **every data slide**, not only slide 5.
- Slide 6 includes stray sequence numerals **04 / 02 / 03 / 01**; visually verify whether these are purposeful or leftover layout elements.

## Corrective actions
1. Replace unsupported slide-3 figures with nonquantitative conceptual dashboard labels.
2. Remove unsourced percent-of-target labels on slide 5 or document explicitly hypothetical denominators.
3. Fix typos and whitespace; verify the actual visual layout at full size, not only extracted text.
4. Add consistent demo disclaimers to every slide containing synthetic data.
5. Test editability of text, diagram objects, and charts directly in Canva.
6. Export and visually compare PDF/PPTX and import into Google Slides for a cross-platform test.
7. Only mark pass after the corrected draft is reviewed and saved, and all six slide images are inspected.
