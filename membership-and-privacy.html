Membership terms

Price and renewal

Membership renews monthly at the price shown in checkout until canceled. The first five founding memberships are $795 per month. Founding members keep that rate while continuously subscribed. Standard membership is $1,495 per month.

Requests and delivery

Email your approved copy, intended audience, brand assets and deadline. You can queue multiple requests. We work on one active request at a time and confirm scope and timing by email. A standard active request targets two business days once all materials are ready. Complex projects may take longer.

Final presentations include editable PowerPoint and PDF. Reply to the delivery email with slide numbers and requested changes for in-scope revisions.

Cancellation and billing help

Cancel before renewal in the Stripe customer portal. Membership continues through the paid billing period. If you cannot access the portal or need help with a charge, email justin@ridgehavencreative.com from your checkout email. Include the invoice date or number.

Privacy notice

We use contact details, project instructions and files to answer inquiries, confirm membership, and design and deliver presentations. Stripe handles payment details. Keep payment card numbers and passwords out of email and project files.

Render hosts this website and Namecheap Private Email hosts our business mailbox. Contact us before sending confidential files so we can agree on restricted sharing. We ask permission before showing customer work in our public portfolio.

For questions about your information, or to request a correction or deletion, email justin@ridgehavencreative.com. We may keep records needed to fulfill legal obligations or resolve a dispute.
