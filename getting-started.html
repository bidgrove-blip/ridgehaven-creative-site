Let's get your first presentation started.

Thank you for joining Ridgehaven Creative. Once your subscription is confirmed, email your first request to justin@ridgehavencreative.com. Please use the email address you entered at checkout so we can match your membership.

What to include

  1. Presentation type: pitch deck, sales proposal, capability statement or business presentation
  2. Goal and audience: what the presentation needs to achieve and who will see it
  3. Content: your approved copy, outline, notes and any reference material
  4. Brand: logo, colors, fonts and examples you like, if available
  5. Format and size: preferred slide count, aspect ratio and PowerPoint requirements
  6. Timing: your desired deadline; we will confirm what is feasible
Email your first request

How requests work

You can queue unlimited presentation design requests. We work on one active request at a time. We acknowledge your request by email and confirm the scope, queue position and expected delivery date. The two-business-day target starts when a standard-size request becomes active and we have all required materials. Final delivery includes an editable PowerPoint file and a PDF. For revisions, reply to your delivery email with the slide numbers and requested changes. In-scope revisions are included.

Manage your subscription

Use our secure billing portal to update payment details, view invoices or cancel at the end of your billing period. Subscription pausing is not currently available.

Keep passwords and payment card details out of project materials. For confidential files, email us first so we can agree on a restricted-access sharing method. If you have not subscribed yet, please start at our membership page.
