Questions, answered

Know what to expect.

What does unlimited queued requests mean?

You may submit as many requests to the queue as needed, but we work on only one request at a time. Requests are handled sequentially.

Are revisions included?

Yes, revisions within the originally agreed scope are included. A substantially new direction may be treated as a new request.

Is two business days guaranteed?

No. Two business days is a target for standard-size requests after complete materials are received. Complexity, revisions and queue volume affect timing.

Can I cancel?

Cancellation and billing terms will be provided before enrollment opens. No subscriptions or charges are active on this preview site.
