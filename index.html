Questions, answered

Know what to expect.

What does unlimited queued requests mean?

You may submit as many requests to the queue as needed, but we work on only one request at a time. Requests are handled sequentially.

Are revisions included?

Yes, revisions within the originally agreed scope are included. A substantially new direction may be treated as a new request.

Is two business days guaranteed?

No. The two-business-day target starts when a standard-size request is active and we have all required materials. Complexity, revisions and queue volume affect timing.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel through the customer billing portal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period.

How do I submit a project?

After subscribing, visit our getting-started page for instructions and email your first request to justin@ridgehavencreative.com.
