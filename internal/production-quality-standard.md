# Ridgehaven Creative — Presentation Production & QA Standard (v1)

Internal working procedure. Do not claim third-party verification or guaranteed turnaround.

## Intake
- Record customer goal, target audience, desired action, deadline, requested editable format, slide count, and brand assets.
- Collect approved source materials, brand fonts, logo, and permissions for third-party assets.
- Confirm scope and queue position. Only one active request per subscription.
- Flag complex research, financial modeling, legal/regulated advice, and rush work for separate scope review; current subscription excludes complex research.

## Content integrity
- Build an outline with one core message per slide and an audience-appropriate narrative.
- Never fabricate customer metrics, client testimonials, case studies, citations, market sizes, or results.
- For sourced factual statements, keep a source log with URL/document title, publication date, accessed date, and slide number.
- Recalculate all percentages, totals, growth rates, and chart values; check units and date ranges.
- Clearly mark assumptions, estimates, illustrative numbers, and client-provided claims; request client confirmation when appropriate.

## Design
- Use consistent hierarchy, alignment, margins, spacing, contrast, and typography.
- Make charts interpretable with labeled axes/units and non-misleading scales.
- Avoid tiny text, illegible footnotes, low-resolution logos, and decorative imagery without purpose.
- Check accessibility: color contrast, reading order where supported, and text alternatives where possible.

## Format-specific acceptance tests
### PowerPoint (.pptx)
- Open the exported file in PowerPoint-compatible viewer and inspect every slide at presentation size.
- Confirm text boxes, charts, tables, and shapes intended to be editable are editable.
- Check missing fonts, clipping, overlap, line breaks, slide ratio, hyperlinks, and embedded media.
### Canva (native editable design; pending account connection and pilot)
- Create or import into Canva, inspect each page, and test text, charts, and graphic editing.
- Verify share permissions, asset licensing, and that the customer can access a duplicate/editable copy.
- Export PDF and compare visual fidelity; do not promise native Canva delivery until pilot passes.
### Google Slides (native editable file; pending connection and pilot)
- Import or create in Google Slides; inspect layouts and test editing and collaborative access.
- Check fonts, charts, links, sharing permissions, and PDF export.
- Do not promise native Slides delivery until pilot passes.

## Final delivery
- Inspect all slides manually and compare with original brief.
- Supply requested source format, PDF preview where applicable, and a concise change summary.
- Explain any limitations or unverified client-provided statements.
- Record delivery date, revision requests, and approval.

## Pilot scorecard (0–5 each)
- Narrative clarity; factual accuracy; visual hierarchy; typography; charts; brand consistency; native editability; export fidelity; delivery readiness.
- Pass threshold: no critical factual errors or broken editing, and each category >=4/5. These are internal proposed thresholds, not audited results.
